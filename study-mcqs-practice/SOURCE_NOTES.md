# Source notes

Source used: the uploaded file `mcqs paper.pdf`, a 32-page scanned English grammar workbook. The PDF had no extractable text layer, so its pages were rendered and OCR was applied to inspect the text. The questions in this website are based on visible workbook definitions, headings, examples and exercises, including nouns, countable nouns, proper nouns, plural nouns, compound nouns, gender, pronouns, possessive pronouns, adjectives, degrees of comparison, past-tense verbs, main/helping verbs, be verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, articles, homophones, anagrams and simile.

## Important OCR limitation
Some scanned handwriting, pencil answers, and printed words are unclear in OCR. The quiz therefore uses clear, directly supported definitions and examples rather than trying to reproduce every filled-in workbook blank. The `sourcePage` field points to the approximate PDF page where the topic or supporting example appears. Explanations are concise paraphrases of the visible material or direct grammatical interpretation of its examples. Please compare against the original PDF if you need exact teacher-specific answer wording.

No external question bank or unrelated subject matter was intentionally added. Questions have four options and one intended correct answer each.
